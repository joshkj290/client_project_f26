Why it matters

Why the Start Matters

First impressions shape the trajectory of a partnership. The starting phase is where abstract project ideas turn into structured, professional commitments.

By establishing clear team contracts, co-creating agendas, and aligning on project scope early, you build psychological safety within your team and trust with your client. Learning to navigate early ambiguity is one of the most valuable professional skills you will gain at UMSI.

Focus & objectives

Starting Goals

  1. Partner Communication

    Draft clear, professional outreach to launch working relationships.

  2. First Meetings

    Structure productive kickoff meetings that define roles and project bounds.

  3. Formalizing Agreements

    Document team commitments and scope agreements shared by all stakeholders.

Check your progress

Ready to Move On?

You are ready for Phase 3 when you can say:

  • We sent a clear, professional introduction to our partner.
  • We held a kickoff meeting with a shared agenda.
  • Everyone agrees on scope, roles, and how we will communicate.
  • Our team agreement or partnership agreement is signed.
