Why it matters

The Value of Navigating Friction

The middle of an engaged learning project is where real growth happens—and where real challenges surface. Requirements shift, team dynamics evolve, clients face unexpected internal constraints, and research data yields surprising results.

The true value of this phase lies in developing resilience, adaptability, and ethical agility. Learning to manage scope creep, navigate mid-project feedback, and re-align technical decisions with human needs are the experiences that distinguish top UMSI graduates.

Focus & objectives

Managing & Executing in the Field

Conduct research, track progress, and maintain clear communication with project sponsors.

  1. Fieldwork & Research Methods

    Apply human-centered design tools to observe contexts and extract insights.

  2. Sponsor & Client Updates

    Maintain transparency with regular progress reporting.

  3. Feedback Loops

    Gather, evaluate, and integrate constructive feedback mid-project.

  4. Navigating Challenges

    Handle scope changes, team conflict, and ethical questions as they come up.

Check your progress

Staying on Track

Throughout this phase, check that:

  • We share progress updates with our partner on a regular schedule.
  • We have collected mid-project feedback and acted on it.
  • We have a plan for handling scope changes and team conflict.
  • We are reflecting on what we are learning, not just what we are building.
