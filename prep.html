Why it matters

Why Preparation Matters

Before writing code, sketching wireframes, or analyzing datasets, engaged learners must cultivate social identity awareness and ethical humility. Entering a community or client environment without examining your own assumptions can lead to misaligned expectations or unintended harm.

Preparing thoughtfully lets you approach partners not as an outside "expert" fixing a problem, but as a collaborative learner working alongside stakeholders.

Focus & objectives

Preparation Goals

  1. Social & Community Awareness

    Examine personal identity, privilege, and community dynamics using civic engagement frameworks.

  2. Expectation & Role Alignment

    Understand program requirements, project commitments, and leadership responsibilities.

  3. Initial Scoping

    Assess organizational needs and evaluate initial project viability.

Check your progress

Ready to Move On?

You are ready for Phase 2 when you can say:

  • I have reflected on my identity, assumptions, and positionality.
  • I understand my program's requirements and my role on the team.
  • I can describe my partner's needs in one or two sentences.
  • My team has a draft scope, roles, and timeline.
